We are pleased to introduce updated versions of the Amazon Kinesis Data Stream and Amazon DynamoDB Stream sources. Built on the FLIP-27 source interface, these newer connectors introduce 7 new features and are compatible with Flink 2.0. The new KinesisStreamsSource replaces the legacy FlinkKinesisConsumer; and the new DynamoDbStreamsSource replaces the legacy FlinkDynamoDBStreamsConsumer.
