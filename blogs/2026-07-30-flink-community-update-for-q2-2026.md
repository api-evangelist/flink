This is the Flink Community Update for Q2 2026, the first in a new quarterly format. Following a discussion with the dev community, the intention going forward is that these updates will appear quarterly rather than monthly, with the aim of highlighting higher-impact topics over a three-month window at a time. This edition covers April through June 2026.
